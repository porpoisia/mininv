Common questions

How do I back up my wardrobe?

Settings › Your Data › Export My Data. Save the file somewhere safe, such as Files or iCloud Drive.

How do I move to a new iPhone, or get my data back?

On the new iPhone, open Settings › Your Data › Restore From an Export… and choose your export. Add What's Missing keeps what's already on the phone; Replace Everything saves a copy of the current data first.

How do I get rid of the sample items?

Tap Remove Samples… on the Inventory, or in Settings › Your Data.

Does MinInv upload my photos or data?

No. Everything stays on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.
