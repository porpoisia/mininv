MinInv

Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

MinInv is a wardrobe inventory app for iPhone. In short: everything you put into MinInv stays on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and the developer has no servers that receive your data.

What MinInv stores, and where

Your items, wishlist, photos, notes, prices and settings are stored only in the app on your iPhone. They are included in your iPhone's own backups (iCloud or a computer) if you have those turned on, under your Apple account, as with any app. The developer cannot see them.

Photos you take or choose are processed on your iPhone, including removing the background. They are never uploaded anywhere by MinInv.

When MinInv uses the internet

MinInv works without an internet connection. It only connects in two cases, both started by you, and it talks directly to the website in question, never through the developer:

  • Pasting a shop link. When you paste a link to a product page, MinInv opens that page to read its title and picture, so you don't have to type them. The website sees an ordinary request for that page, as it would from a browser.
  • Checking online listings. For wishlist items with a link to a supported second-hand selling site, MinInv can check that listing to show whether it has sold. You can turn this off at any time in Settings › Wishlist › Check Online Listings; when it's off, no checks are made.

Those websites have their own privacy policies. MinInv sends them nothing except the request for the page you linked.

Camera and photos

MinInv asks for camera access only so you can photograph your items. Photos you pick from your library are chosen through Apple's photo picker, so MinInv only receives the photos you select.

Exports

Settings › Your Data › Export My Data creates a file of your data and photos. It goes only where you choose to save or send it. Restoring reads a file you choose.

Children

MinInv collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the MinInv app from your iPhone deletes everything it stored. Exports you saved elsewhere stay where you saved them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
